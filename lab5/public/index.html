Featured Poetry

Moonlight

Under the quiet moon,
I found a piece of me,
hiding between the stars,
waiting to be free.

Unsaid Words

Some words remain silent,
yet speak louder than sound,
living inside our hearts,
where they can always be found.

Journey

The road may twist,
the skies may change,
but every step we take
makes the journey strange.
